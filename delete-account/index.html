Delete your StaConPic account

You can delete your StaConPic account at any time, in the app or by email. StaConPic is made by SHEETS AND APPS, LLC.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open StaConPic and tap Settings.
  2. Scroll down to Delete account and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

If you are the only Manager of a team that has other people, the app asks you to make someone else a Manager first (Settings → Team).

By email

If you can't use the app, email support@staconpic.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my StaConPic account". We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted, and what is kept

What happens
Your sign-in, email address, name and cell phone number Deleted
Your team memberships and project access Deleted
Invitations waiting for your email address Deleted
Photos you took for your team Kept by your team, because they are the company's project records. Your name stays on photos already stamped.
Photos on your phone that hadn't uploaded Never uploaded; they stay on the phone until you delete them or uninstall the app

To have photos you took deleted too, ask a Manager on your team, who can delete them in the app. Deleted photos are removed for good after 30 days.

To delete a whole team and all its photos, a Manager emails support@staconpic.com. The team then has 90 days to export its photos before everything is deleted.
