6. Location, station and offset accuracy

Locations, elevations, camera directions, stations and offsets in StaConPic are estimates for documentation, not a survey.

  • They come from the phone's GPS, compass and sensors, which can be off by several metres or more, especially near buildings, trees, steel or heavy equipment. The app records the GPS accuracy it received.
  • Stations and offsets are only as good as the alignment files a Manager imports and the start stations entered.
  • Don't use StaConPic values for layout, staking, pay quantities, legal boundaries or safety decisions. Use a licensed surveyor or proper survey equipment for those.

Dates and times come from the phone's clock and time zone.
