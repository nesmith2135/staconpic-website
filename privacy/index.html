Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

1. Who we are

StaConPic is a construction photo app made by HealthLife Solutions, LLC ("we", "us"), a Florida limited liability company. This policy explains what the StaConPic app and its online service collect, why, and who can see it.

StaConPic is used by companies and their teams. Your company (the "team") decides who joins, which projects exist and what is asked about each photo. The photos and details you take belong to your team. We store them and keep them secure on the team's behalf; we don't sell them or use them for advertising.

This policy covers the Android and iPhone apps and any StaConPic website. It does not cover other apps or sites you open from StaConPic, such as email or file-sharing apps.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to take, stamp, store and share inspection photos. We don't use advertising, analytics or crash-tracking services.

Information Where it comes from Why
Email address You, when you sign in To send sign-in codes and identify you on your team
Name Your Manager, when inviting you or later Shown on your photos and to your team
Cell phone number (optional) Your Manager, if they enter it Seen only by you and your Managers. Not used yet; later it may be used to send sign-in codes by text message
Team and role (Manager or Inspector) Your team's Managers To decide what you can see and change
Photos (stamped copy, original and thumbnail) Your phone's camera The main purpose of the app
Photo location: latitude, longitude, GPS accuracy, elevation Your phone's GPS, only while the camera screen is open Printed on the photo and used for station, offset and the map
Camera direction and phone orientation Your phone's compass and motion sensors, while the camera screen is open Printed on the photo and shown on the map
Date, time and time zone of each photo Your phone Printed on the photo and used for filters
Comments, dropdown and checkbox answers You Your team's photo details
Edit and delete history (who, when) The app So your team can see who changed a photo
Project files, such as KMZ / KML alignments Your team's Managers To work out stations and offsets
Basic technical data: IP address, app version, request times Sent automatically when the app connects To deliver the service, app updates and maps, and to keep it secure

We do not track your location in the background or when the camera screen is closed.

3. Who can see your information

Only people on your team, and only what their role allows. Nobody outside your team sees your photos unless someone on your team shares them.

Who What they can see
Inspectors on your team The team's photos and details, team member names, the team's projects
Managers on your team Everything Inspectors see, plus team email addresses and cell phone numbers, deleted photos in the recycle bin and edit history
People a team member shares with Only the photos or export files that were shared, through a link that stops working after 7 days or a file sent outside the app
Us Only when needed to run, support or secure the service, or when the law requires it

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect people's safety, or as part of a sale or merger of the business, in which case this policy would continue to apply.

5. Phone permissions

The app asks for each permission when it first needs it. You can turn any of them off in your phone's settings; the matching feature stops working.

Permission Used for If you turn it off
Camera Taking inspection photos You can't take photos in the app
Location (while using the app) Recording where each photo was taken Photos have no location, station or map pin
Motion sensors Recording which way the camera points Photos have no camera direction
Photos and media Saving copies to your phone's gallery, if you turn that on in Settings Copies aren't saved to your gallery
Files Picking alignment files and an export folder You can't import alignments or save exports to a folder

The app never records audio or video.

6. How long we keep data, and deleting your account

We keep your team's photos and details for as long as the team uses StaConPic, because they are often project records the company must keep.

Data How long
Photos and details Until your team deletes them or closes its account
Deleted photos (recycle bin) 30 days, then removed for good
Shared export files and links 7 days
Your account (email, name, cell phone number) Until you or your team removes it
Copies on your phone Until you delete them or uninstall the app
Server logs A short time, as kept by our providers
After a team closes its account 90 days, so the team can export its photos, then deleted

Deleting your account

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account.
  • Or email support@staconpic.com from the address you sign in with. See Delete your account for details.

We delete your account within 30 days. Photos you took for your team stay with the team, because they are the company's project records; your name stays on photos already stamped. A Manager can delete those photos, or you can ask your team to.

If you leave a team, its Managers remove you and you lose access to its photos right away.

7. Security, children, your rights and contact

Security. Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with our providers' security controls. Each team can only reach its own data, and sign-in uses one-time email codes or a password you choose, which is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Children. StaConPic is a work tool for adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, download or delete your personal information. To ask, email support@staconpic.com. Because your team controls its photos, we may pass photo requests to your team's Managers. We won't treat you differently for making a request.

Changes. If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
