StaConPic

A station-based construction photo documentation system.

StaConPic is an app for construction inspectors. Every photo is stamped with the project, date and time, inspector, GPS location, and the station and offset along the project's alignment, then uploaded to the team's shared gallery.

Station and offset

Import the project's KMZ or KML centerlines and each photo is stamped with its station and offset.

Project details

Managers set the dropdowns and checkboxes inspectors fill in after each photo.

Works without signal

Photos are saved on the phone and upload when there's coverage.

Review and export

Filter, map and edit the team's photos, and export them with a spreadsheet.

StaConPic is in a private pilot. For questions or access, email support@staconpic.com.
